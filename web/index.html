Turing's ACE, as proposed in 1945

The Automatic Computing Engine of Alan Turing's report Proposed Electronic Calculator, run in your browser: its delay lines and temporary storages, its two types of instruction, and his instruction tables written in his popular form, TS 6 - TS 2, B, INDEXIN, ADD 'A'. The machine was never built. It is the same Rust program as the command line, compiled to WebAssembly.
